System Reference

Quick cheat sheet for resolving encounters during our sessions.

🔄 Reverse Hit Points (Arousal) Instead of losing HP down to 0, your Arousal starts at 0 and climbs up. Your "Arousal Maximum" is the breaking point of pleasure your character can take before losing control.
⚔️ Sexual Damage (Stimulation) Instead of using physical weapons to slash or pierce, attacks are made via Sexual Advances (sensory words, spellcasting, or suggestive touch). Success deals Stimulation, raising the target's Arousal.
🛡️ The New Armor (AC + Inhibition) To see if a seductive or sensory advance successfully lands, the attacker rolls a d20 against the target's Armor Class (AC) + Inhibition (their raw mental fortitude to stay collected).
⚠️ Edging & Climaxing If your Arousal hits its maximum, you gain the Edging condition. At the start of your turn, roll a d20 + Inhibition:
  • 3 Successes (15+): You stabilize and calm down.
  • 3 Failures: You Climax and become incapacitated for a brief period while recovering.
