Chamber Rules

Quick reference for resolving romantic or alluring encounters.

🔄 Reverse Hit Points (Arousal) Instead of dropping down to zero health, your score starts at 0 and goes up. The Arousal Maximum is how much sensory pleasure you can maintain before losing composure.
⚔️ Flirtatious Damage (Stimulation)

Instead of physical weapons, checks are delivered via sensory words, spellcasting magic, or subtle touch.

Success increases the target's total Arousal. When someone successfully makes an advance against you, they roll dice to see how intense it is. That number is Stimulation.

🛡️ Composure (AC + Inhibition) To successfully bypass a target's mental defenses with an advance, roll a d20 against their Armor Class (AC) + Inhibition modifier.
⚠️ Edging & Climaxing Reaching max Arousal triggers the Edging state. At the start of your turn, roll a d20 + Inhibition:
  • 3 Successes (15+): You regain focus and settle down.
  • 3 Failures: You Climax, leaving you beautifully incapacitated for a brief moment.
